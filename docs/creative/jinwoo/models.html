THE NEXT HANDOFF

Bring the models into the project.

The files let us inspect the skeletons, adjust the materials, and author the command and kneeling animations.

  1. 01

    Download the free package

    Open the selected model's source page and sign in to Sketchfab. Choose GLB or glTF if offered, or the original FBX archive. Keep textures with the model.

  2. 02

    Keep each package together

    Place the Jin-Woo download in assets/incoming/jinwoo/ and Igris in assets/incoming/igris/. ZIP archives can remain intact for the first inspection.

  3. 03

    Inspect, then animate

    After download, we will inspect the skeletons, textures, and available clips, then test shoulder, finger, coat, and knee deformation. We can author the command and response once the rigs are usable.
